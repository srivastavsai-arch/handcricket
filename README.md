# Hand Cricket

The street game of hand cricket, playable in your browser. Call the toss, pick your numbers, and outsmart an AI that learns how you play.

**Play it here: https://handcricketvas.vercel.app/**

## Why it's fun

- Play with your camera. Hold up your fingers and your hand becomes the controller.
- Or just tap the numbers. Keyboard works too.
- A real match flow: toss, choose bat or bowl, two innings, and a target chase.
- An adaptive AI opponent with easy, medium, and hard difficulties.
- Live score, ball history, and a chase tracker.
- Made for phones, plays great on desktop.

## Camera mode

Choose Play with Camera and show a hand gesture for your number. Hold it steady until it locks in, then move your hand away for the next ball.

| Number | Gesture |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Closed fist |
| 1 | Index finger |
| 2 | Index + middle |
| 3 | Index + middle + ring |
| 4 | Index + middle + ring + pinky |
| 5 | All five fingers |
| 6 | Thumb only |
| 7 | Thumb + index |
| 8 | Thumb + index + middle |
| 9 | Thumb + pinky |
| 10 | Thumb + index + pinky |

## Privacy

Your camera is only used to read your gesture while you play. Nothing is recorded or stored. Details in the [Privacy Policy](https://handcricketvas.vercel.app/privacy.html).
