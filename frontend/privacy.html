HAND CRICKET

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Overview

Hand Cricket is a browser game based on the traditional hand-gesture street game. It is designed privacy-first: gameplay and hand-gesture recognition run on your own device, inside your web browser. Using the camera is optional — the full game can be played by tapping the number pad.

2. Camera Access

Your browser only asks for camera permission after you choose Play with Camera. The camera is used for one purpose only: real-time hand-gesture recognition while you play. If you deny or dismiss the permission request, nothing breaks — you can keep playing with taps.

3. Camera and Visual Data

The Hand Cricket game does not upload, record, or store your camera footage or visual hand data. Specifically, the game does not collect or keep any database of camera footage, hand images, hand landmarks, gesture recordings, or video recordings. The game cannot identify who you are from your hand.

4. Local Processing

Gesture recognition happens locally in your browser on your device. Each camera frame is analysed in temporary browser memory to detect the current hand gesture, and once a frame is no longer needed for recognition it is discarded as part of normal browser processing.

5. Gameplay Data

Scores, moves, and match progress are held in temporary page memory for your current session only, so the game can display the scoreboard and ball history. Leaving or reloading the page clears them. Depending on how the site is hosted, starting a match may send basic match-setup information (such as a session id and difficulty choice) to the host server, which keeps it in temporary server memory for that session. No camera footage or visual hand data is ever included in such requests.

6. Data Storage

The game does not use accounts, cookies, or browser storage to persist your data. It stores nothing on your device beyond what your browser needs to display the current page, and it maintains no remote database of players or gameplay.

7. Third Party Services

The game itself includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Two external dependencies are loaded over the network as part of normal website delivery:

  • jsDelivr CDN — delivers the MediaPipe hand-tracking library, and only after you opt into Camera Mode.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the site typefaces when pages load.

Requests to these providers are subject to their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Hand Cricket has no chat, accounts, or data collection features. It is a self-contained game suited to general audiences; younger players should use the camera feature with a parent or guardian's permission.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website's functionality changes. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its revision date.
